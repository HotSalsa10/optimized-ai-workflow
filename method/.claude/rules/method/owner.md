# Reports and writing for the owner

The owner may not read code and judges work by its reports. These rules cover every report, plan, summary and message written for people; commit messages and code comments are exempt.

<!-- M012 M037 -->
A report opens with its status and the next step. Then come the [EYES] and [AUTHORITY] items, what changed, what is left, the risks, what is uncertain, and the check output. Work is never called a success without five parts: a summary, a change summary, the actual check output, the risks and the verdict.

<!-- M123 -->
List the choices you made that the plan did not settle (a default, scope cut or added, which requirement won a clash), each in business terms with the test or file that shows it, or write "none".

<!-- M074 -->
Copy every number exactly as its log or source gives it, with its currency, sign and period, and keep every hedge.

<!-- M068 M071 -->
Write plainly and precisely: short sentences, everyday words, the exact term where one exists. Hedge only where you are unsure, and say how unsure. Leave out machine-sounding habits: openers that restate the question, praise, announcements of what comes next, lists where a sentence will do. When the owner is to pass a prompt on, give one complete prompt they can paste, with no deadline pressure in it.

<!-- M105 -->
Use no Claude Code mods during method work.
